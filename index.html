        Our story

        Four generations. One standard.

        It started with a neighborhood plumbing and hardware store and a simple rule from founder Alex Joseph Danboise: deliver the best products and service, without compromise. A hundred years later, that rule still decides how we bid, build and answer the phone.

        Woman-owned and family-led. DMI is run by Alex's granddaughter Carol Danboise Gatewood and great-granddaughter Amy Gatewood Gendjar.

        1. 1925

          A.J. Danboise & Son opens

          Alex Joseph Danboise opens a neighborhood plumbing and hardware store built on quality and customer service.

        2. Decades

          The name becomes a promise

          Through three generations, "Danboise" becomes synonymous with trust and craftsmanship across Metro Detroit.

        3. 2004

          Danboise Mechanical Inc. is formed

          The commercial division branches off to focus on industrial and commercial plumbing, HVAC and electrical work.

        4. 2025

          100 years, 75+ professionals

          A fourth-generation, WBENC-certified company with a technologically advanced facility, a modern fleet and live dispatch around the clock.
